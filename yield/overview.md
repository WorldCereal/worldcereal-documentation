# Yield Modelling

The Agriculture Remotely-sensed Yield Algorithm (ARYA) is an Earth Observation (EO)-based modelling framework designed to provide timely and quantitative forecasts of wheat yield at national and subnational levels.

This section introduces the ARYA yield modelling approach and explains how the WorldCereal products can support crop-specific yield forecasting, including:
- Background context on the ARYA algorithm
- Integration of the WorldCereal products
- Practical guidelines to get started
- Requirements for applying te workflow in new regions
